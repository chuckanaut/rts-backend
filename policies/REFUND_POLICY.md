Target Warfare - Refund & Returns Policy (DRAFT)

Physical goods (mugs, tumblers)
- Custom printed items are produced to order using customer-provided artwork. Because these are custom prints, refunds are issued only when: (a) the item arrives damaged, (b) the print is defective, or (c) the order shipped does not match the confirmed order.
- If a customer provides artwork that fails moderation (copyright or prohibited content), we will notify the customer and cancel the order with a full refund.
- To request a refund, contact support@example.com within 14 days of delivery and provide order number and photos.

Non-refundable items
- Custom print items altered by the customer (uploaded design) are not refundable for change-of-mind reasons.

Digital goods
- Digital entitlements and in-game cosmetics are non-refundable through the app store. Refunds follow platform policies (Google Play).

Processing refunds
- Refunds are processed via Stripe; allow 5–10 business days for the payment to return to the original payment method.
